HOEJABIHAVEN takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what we don't do with it. We collect the bare minimum necessary to operate this platform.
01Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to process your order and deliver access:

  • Your Telegram username or contact information you provide when messaging us

We do not collect your real name, physical address, date of birth, or government ID. We do not store full payment card details — payment information is handled directly in Telegram DMs.

02How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to:

  • Process your membership purchase and deliver vault access
  • Respond to support requests and resolve access issues
  • Process tier upgrades if you request one

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond fulfilling your order.

03Information We Do Not Share

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third party for any reason, including advertising, analytics, or marketing purposes. Your data stays between you and HOEJABIHAVEN, full stop.

04Anonymous Payments

Cryptocurrency payments are inherently anonymous. We accept BTC, ETH, USDT and other cryptocurrencies precisely because they allow members to transact without exposing personal financial information. No payment processor is given your browsing history or platform activity.

05Data Retention

Order records (tier purchased, reference ID, Telegram contact) are retained for the minimum period necessary to support your access and handle legitimate upgrade or dispute requests. If you would like your records deleted, contact us via Telegram and we will remove them within 7 business days.

06Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be noted on this page. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of HOEJABIHAVEN after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
