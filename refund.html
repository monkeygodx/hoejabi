All memberships on HOEJABIHAVEN are one-time, lifetime access purchases for digital content. Please read this policy carefully before completing your purchase.
01No Refunds on Digital Access
All sales are final. Due to the immediate and digital nature of membership access, we do not issue refunds once vault access has been delivered.

By completing payment and receiving access to the HOEJABIHAVEN vault, you acknowledge and agree that no refund will be issued under any circumstances once access has been granted. This is consistent with standard practices for digital content platforms.

Before purchasing, we encourage you to review the features of each tier carefully on our membership page to ensure you select the right tier for your needs.

02Exception: Access Not Delivered

The only exception to our no-refund policy is if vault access was not delivered within a reasonable time after payment was confirmed. If you completed payment and did not receive access, please contact us immediately via Telegram.

  • Contact @killsaints on Telegram with your payment reference and transaction details
  • Include the Reference ID from your order confirmation page if available
  • Undelivered-access disputes must be raised within 48 hours of payment confirmation

We will investigate and either deliver your access or, where delivery is genuinely not possible, issue a full refund. We stand behind every order.

03Tier Upgrades

If you purchased a lower tier and wish to upgrade, we offer upgrade pricing. Contact @killsaints on Telegram with your existing access details. Upgrade fees are the difference between your current tier and the higher tier — you will not be charged twice for the same content.

Downgrades are not available and no partial refund is issued for the difference between tiers.

04Chargebacks and Disputes

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute after vault access has been delivered is considered a violation of our Terms of Service and will result in immediate, permanent termination of your membership with no possibility of reinstatement.

If you have a legitimate concern about your purchase, please contact us directly before initiating a dispute. We are responsive and will work with you to resolve the issue. Chargebacks are a last resort — not a substitute for communication.
